# Evidence Judge v0 Baseline

## Dataset

`claim_evidence_v1` contains 120 reviewed controlled pairs across all six benchmark documents: 40 SUPPORTED, 40 PARTIAL, and 40 UNSUPPORTED. It is derived from the first human-reviewed retrieval gold span of each of the 40 reviewed retrieval cases. Dataset SHA256: `bb6f9924b60db6d247fe04bc429cdc07a22e6e417d28e6fb4065335b7bb52609`.

The construction deliberately preserves high lexical overlap for qualifier, entity, numeric, and temporal mismatch negatives, while adding a fixed unsupported-risk-inference control. Gold labels were generated by fixed rules before evaluation and were not adjusted from Judge predictions.

## Failure analysis

The dominant failures are missing qualifiers (37) and wrong entities (23), followed by numeric mismatch (4), semantic overlap but insufficient evidence (4), and temporal mismatch (1). The v0 overlap heuristic generally treats lexical similarity as sufficiency and rarely requests human review.

Next-phase hypotheses, not implemented here: explicitly check entity/party consistency; extract and compare numeric and temporal qualifiers; separate factual overlap from complete claim entailment; and introduce calibrated abstention for missing conditions. These hypotheses require their own evaluation before changing Judge behavior.
